Privacy Policy

Hourglass Pomodoro · Last updated: 25 October 2026

1. Controller

The data controller is Sebastián Zaruha, Madrid, Spain.
Contact: sebastianzaruha@gmail.com

2. About this app

Hourglass Pomodoro is a free timer / countdown app for Android. It is distributed as an APK (for example via GitHub or Google Drive). It requires no account, shows no ads and does not connect to any server of ours.

3. Data we process (and data we don’t)

This app does not collect or send personal data off your device. In particular:

  • There is no sign-up or login.
  • There are no ads and no analytics.
  • No data is sent to the Internet or to third parties.

On your own phone the app may store local preferences (for example light/dark theme and language). If you choose “Automatic”, it follows the system theme and language settings. That information stays on the device and is not transmitted.

4. System permissions

The app may request Android permissions it needs to work, and uses them only for that:

  • Notifications: to show the countdown and alert you when a session or phase ends, even with the app closed.
  • Display over other apps (optional): for the floating timer window. If you don’t grant it, the app still works.
  • Vibration and the motion sensor (accelerometer): for vibration and for tilting, laying down or flipping the hourglass. Motion is processed instantly on the device and is neither stored nor sent.

You can revoke any permission in Android settings at any time.

5. Purpose and legal basis

Because the app does not collect personal data or send it off the device, the controller does not process personal data through it. Local preferences and permissions are only used so that the app works the way you choose.

6. Retention

Preferences are kept on your device until you clear them or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes those local data with the app (except what Android may keep in its own backups).

7. Recipients and transfers

No personal data is shared with third parties or transferred internationally through this app, because no data is sent off the device.

8. Children

The app is not specifically directed at children under 14 and does not collect identifying data. Parents or guardians with questions can email us.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may exercise rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD: www.aepd.es).

Because we do not store personal data on our own servers, you can effectively exercise several of these rights by clearing preferences or uninstalling the app. For any request: sebastianzaruha@gmail.com. We will reply within the legal time limit (at most one month).

10. Changes

If the app changes (for example analytics, ads or a backend), we will update this policy and the date. Please check it from time to time.
