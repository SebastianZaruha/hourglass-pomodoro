Privacy policy

Hourglass Pomodoro · Last updated: 25 October 2026

1. Controller

The data controller is Sebastián Zaruha, Madrid (Spain).
Contact: sebastianzaruha+apps@gmail.com

2. What Hourglass Pomodoro is

Hourglass Pomodoro (formerly “Reloj de arena”) is a free timer / countdown offered through two channels:

  • Android app (APK): distributed e.g. via GitHub or Google Drive.
  • Web version: available in your browser.

Neither channel requires an account or registration, and neither shows ads.

3. Data we process, by channel

Android app (APK): it does not collect or send personal data outside your device.

  • No sign-up or login.
  • No ads and no analytics.
  • No data is sent to the Internet or to third parties.

On your phone the app may store local preferences (light/dark theme and language). With “Automatic” it follows your system settings. This information stays on the device and is not transmitted.

Web version: it also stores your theme and language preferences and your cookie choice locally in your browser. In addition, it may use the analytics tools described in section 4.

4. Analytics (web version only)

  • Vercel Web Analytics (Vercel Inc., USA): cookieless visit statistics. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in understanding aggregate site usage. Transfers to the USA rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and/or the Data Privacy Framework, as stated by Vercel.
  • Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC): uses cookies and identifiers. Only enabled if you accept in the cookie banner. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time via “Cookie settings”. More info: Google’s privacy policy.

5. Cookies

See the Cookie policy. Theme and language preferences are strictly necessary and do not require consent.

6. System permissions (Android app)

The app may request Android permissions needed to work, for example:

  • Notifications: to alert you when the timer ends.
  • Display over other apps / floating window: if you use that feature.

You can deny or revoke these permissions in Android settings. Denying them may limit features but never causes data to be sent.

7. Legal basis

Preferences and permissions you enable are processed to provide the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). On the web, cookieless analytics relies on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) and Google Analytics on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). Your information is not used for advertising or profiling.

8. Retention

Preferences are kept on your device or browser until you delete them or uninstall the app. Your cookie choice is stored for up to about 12 months, after which you will be asked again. Analytics data is retained according to each provider’s settings and policies.

9. Recipients and transfers

Android app: no data is shared with third parties and there are no international transfers, since no data leaves the device.

Web version: usage data may be accessed by Vercel Inc. (hosting and Web Analytics) and, only with your consent, Google (Google Analytics 4). Both may involve transfers to the USA, covered by Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the Data Privacy Framework.

10. Children

Hourglass Pomodoro is not specifically aimed at children under 14 and does not collect identifying data. If you are a parent or guardian with questions, write to the contact email.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the Spanish LOPDGDD you may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

On the web you can accept, reject or withdraw consent to analytics cookies at any time via “Cookie settings” or your browser. In the app, you can clear preferences or uninstall it. For any request: sebastianzaruha+apps@gmail.com. We will reply within the legal deadline (one month at most).

12. Changes

If Hourglass Pomodoro changes (e.g. new tools, ads or a server), we will update this policy with a new date. We recommend checking it from time to time.
