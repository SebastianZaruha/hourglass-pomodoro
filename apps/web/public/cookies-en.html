Cookie policy — Hourglass Pomodoro

Controller: Sebastián Zaruha, Madrid (Spain). Contact: sebastianzaruha+apps@gmail.com

What they are

Cookies and similar technologies store or read information on your device.

Types we use

  1. Strictly necessary / technical (no consent required): theme and language preference; storing your cookie choice.
  2. Analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4): only with consent. They measure visits, pages and traffic sources.
  3. Vercel Web Analytics: does not use cookies; only with consent. See the privacy policy.

Cookie and storage table

Name Owner Purpose Duration

_ga, _ga_* Google Analytics 4 Visit analytics (only if you accept) Up to 24 months (per Google’s settings)

Vercel Web Analytics does not use cookies and loads only if you accept. Google’s privacy details: Google Privacy Policy.

How to manage them

In the banner you can choose Accept, Reject or Configure. You can change your choice at any time via “Cookie settings” in the footer. You can also manage or delete them in your browser settings.

More detail

See the Privacy policy, which explains your rights. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish DPA, AEPD (www.aepd.es).
