3. Data we process

The app does not create user accounts and does not ask for your name, email or password to use it.

Depending on your choice:

3.1 Without analytics / stability consent

Only local technical data needed for the timer (preferences — theme, language, sound, vibration, duration and the hourglass material: sand, rain, honey or snow — and timers) that stay on your device. The material is not included in analytics events.

3.2 With consent (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics)

A single consent covers both analytics and crash reports.

Through Google Firebase we may process:

  • Installation / device identifiers (e.g. Firebase Installation ID, Android technical IDs).
  • App usage data (aggregated events: opens, screens, basic interactions).
  • Device technical information (model, OS version, language, approximate network data).
  • Crash reports (Crashlytics): crash stacks and app state at failure time.

There are no browser cookies in the app; device and SDK identifiers may still be used and are treated under privacy / ePrivacy rules.
